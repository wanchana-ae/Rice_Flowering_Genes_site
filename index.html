Read before interpreting. The VCF is raw (unfiltered): genotypes with DP<5 or GQ<20 are set to missing, and nothing else is filtered. All p-values are exploratory and unadjusted for population structure: photosensitivity is itself strongly structured across rice sub-populations, so an association here can reflect ancestry rather than the gene. Multiple-testing correction (Benjamini-Hochberg q) is across all SNPs/genes tested. Phenotype rules: label asterisks ignored; W-codes appearing more than once in the sheet (8 codes, 16 rows) removed entirely; 3 accessions with a non-numeric flowering date and 1 with an unclear photosensitivity label are kept only for the analysis they have data for.

4 · Accession groups from each gene's SNPs

For each gene the SNPs (MAF ≥ 0.02, call rate ≥ 0.8) were reduced by PCA and clustered (Ward); k = 2–6 was chosen by silhouette with every group ≥ 10 accessions. Groups are defined from genotypes only, then tested against flowering date (ANOVA, η² = share of variance) and photosensitivity (chi-square or permutation; Cramér's V). "COMBINED" pools the SNPs of all genes, each gene weighted equally. A silhouette below 0.25 would indicate weak group structure.
